# Home Credit Product Knowledge Base — Loan Against Property (LAP)

## 1. Product Summary
- **Product Name**: Home Credit Loan Against Property (LAP)
- **Target Audience**: Existing valued Home Credit customers seeking secured business or personal funds.
- **Nature of Offer**: Preliminary pre-qualification check (not a final loan sanction or approval).
- **Maximum Loan Amount**: ₹75,00,000 (Seventy-Five Lakh Rupees).

## 2. Eligibility Criteria & Guidelines

### Property Classification
- **Acceptable**: Residential (apartments, independent houses, villas), Commercial (shops, office spaces), Industrial units.
- **Unacceptable**: Agricultural properties (farmland, agricultural plantations, rural farm holdings).
- **Rule**: If a customer's property is Agricultural, immediately terminate qualification with polite explanation.

### Property Ownership
- **Sole Ownership**: Fully accepted.
- **Joint Ownership**: Fully accepted. Joint owners can apply as co-applicants.

### Documentation Requirements
- **Mandatory**: Original property title deeds, parent documents, and registered sale deeds must be available for physical legal verification.
- **Unacceptable**: Cases where only photocopies or certified copies exist because originals are missing, pledged elsewhere, or disputed.
- **Possession vs Location**: Having documents stored securely at home, in a bank locker, or with an advocate satisfies the requirement, provided they are retrievable for verification.

### Loan Amount Guidelines
- Minimum: Determined case-by-case (typically ₹5 Lakhs).
- Maximum Cap: ₹75,00,000 (₹75 Lakhs).
- If customer requests > ₹75 Lakhs: Present alternative of capping requested loan at ₹75 Lakhs. If customer accepts ₹75 Lakhs, proceed. If declined, mark as Maximum Amount Not Accepted.

### Occupation & Income Mode
- **Occupation**: Both Salaried employees and Self-employed individuals/business owners are eligible.
- **Mode of Income Receipt**: Must be credited directly into a verified bank account.
- **Unacceptable**: Cash-in-hand income without direct bank credit is disqualified.

### Estimated Market Value
- Capture customer's estimate of the current property value in INR.
- **Important**: Home Credit does NOT specify an arbitrary minimum market value threshold for this initial qualification screening. Do not disqualify on estimated value.

### Repayment Tenure
- Minimum Permitted: 3 Years (36 Months).
- Maximum Permitted: 15 Years (180 Months).
- Any tenure strictly < 3 years or > 15 years is not eligible under this product scheme.

### Existing Loans & Balance Transfers
- If the customer currently has an active loan against this specific property or desires an EMI-reduction balance transfer, route immediately to the **Loan Transfer Specialist** desk.
- Do not continue standard fresh LAP qualification questions.

### Interest Rates & Pricing
- Interest rates are personalized following legal scrutiny and property valuation by technical teams.
- Voice agents MUST NEVER quote arbitrary interest rates, APRs, or processing charges. Always defer to the senior loan specialist.
